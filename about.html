About 49th Cork

Scouting in Ballincollig since 1975.

Our Group

49th Cork Ballincollig Scout Group is based at the Scout Hall on Station Road in Ballincollig, Co. Cork. We give young people opportunities for adventure, friendship, teamwork, personal development and skills for life.

Our home

Our Scout Hall is at the heart of the Group and is the base for weekly section meetings and many Group activities.

Part of Scouting Ireland

49th Cork is part of Scouting Ireland and the wider worldwide Scout movement.
